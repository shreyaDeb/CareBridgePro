How dispatch works for staff

Less searching.
More doing.

You don't browse jobs or compete for work. Requests come to your department, and the on-call person is assigned — with the customer details up front.

Routed by department

Each request is categorised by the customer's need and routed straight to the right team.

Assigned to on-call

Whoever is on shift for that department gets the ticket automatically — no queue to fight.

Timestamps per job

Accept, arrival, and completion are logged. Your record reflects the work you actually did.

Backup when you need it

Flag a job for backup and your supervisor reassigns. The customer still gets a clear update.

A calmer way to work

From dispatch to close.

Simple steps keep the shift clear — from the moment a request lands to the moment your department closes it.
